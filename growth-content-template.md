# Iconia AI — Organic Growth Content Template

## Goal
Create useful, search-intent pages that lead naturally to Iconia AI without using the owner's personal social accounts.

## Page structure
1. Clear search-intent title
2. Short answer to the visitor's question
3. Visual examples or use cases
4. What Iconia AI can do
5. Simple 3-step workflow
6. CTA: try Iconia AI
7. FAQ with genuinely useful answers
8. Internal links to relevant existing pages

## Quality rules
- Do not mass-produce near-duplicate pages.
- Do not invent search volume, reviews, customers, or performance claims.
- Do not use misleading clickbait.
- Keep the content useful even if the visitor does not purchase.
- Only publish after checking the page renders correctly.

## Distribution
- Organic search is the primary channel.
- Dedicated Iconia AI social accounts may be used later.
- Never publish through the owner's personal X/Instagram accounts.

## Measurement
Track: landing page views, CTA clicks, image generations, pricing views, checkout starts, completed purchases.

## Iteration
Prioritize pages with impressions/visits and weak CTA conversion. Expand topics that bring qualified visitors; retire pages that receive no meaningful traffic after a reasonable test period.
